november 3, 2026 ? agent swarms

when two players take the same shot.

The miss I keep coming back to is simple to describe and messy to clean up. Growth and sales both pick up the same lead list. Each one does a competent job. The person on the other end hears from the company twice, and nobody can say which conversation counts. That is not a model failure. Both players took the same shot because the board did not show that the shot had already been taken.

I had met a version of this before the job was agents. On the F1TENTH car, LIDAR, the IMU, and the camera each reported a version of the track. Any one of them could look reasonable alone. The car still drove the wrong picture when they stopped agreeing, and it did so faster than we could argue about which sensor to trust. The stack was not short on data. It was short on a single picture.

The routing project was the quieter version of the same miss. Three algorithms can return three routes. Three answers are a demo. A dispatcher needs one route, under real constraints, on a map they can look at without me in the room. If they cannot check it, they will ask for it again, and the new route will not be the one the last decision used.

You can walk the same miss on this site, one step at a time. Research confirms a segment, product builds against it, then growth and sales overlap until operations makes one contact log the record everyone else has to read. I would rather show that than ask you to take the word “coordination” on faith. A swarm holds when the next player can see the shot that was already taken.
