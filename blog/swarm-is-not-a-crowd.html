september 22, 2026 ? agent swarms

a swarm is not a crowd of chatbots.

People hear “agent swarm” and picture a lot of chat windows running at once. That picture is why the explanation fails. A crowd of copies does the same job louder. A swarm, the way I use the word at VibeGrow, is a set of agents with different jobs and one objective.

The jobs I work across are research, product, growth, sales, and operations. Research is supposed to find a narrow slice of demand. Product is supposed to build against that slice. Growth and sales are supposed to reach the people in it. Operations is supposed to keep a record of what has already been decided, so the next step does not restart the argument. Not one of them is a general chatbot waiting for a prompt. Each one is there for a lane.

VibeGrow is a pre-launch startup. The product is agent swarms that can research, build, and operate a business. My piece is the coordination. That means orchestration so the agents can share state, and a handoff so a downstream agent acts on the current decision rather than one that has already been revised. More agents do not make the system smarter. They make agreement harder, which is the part worth building.

If you only remember one distinction, make it this. A chatbot answers the person in front of it. A swarm is finished only when the separate lanes still describe the same business. That is a different test, and it fails in a different place. The next note is about what it means for those agents to be in the work at the same time.
